## 1. Introduction and Architectural Overview
This specification consolidates requirements for the Evergreen Integrated Library System (ILS) derived from raw stakeholder inputs, including librarian interviews, technical subcommittee meeting minutes, system administrator consultations, and email discussions. All requirements have been normalized, checked for semantic deduplication, categorized, and traced to their source documentation.

### Priority Scale Explanation (MoSCoW Method - OS6)

The requirements in this specification are prioritized using the internationally recognized MoSCoW method:

1. **Must Have (P1 - Critical)**:
   - Non-negotiable capabilities essential for the fundamental operation, security, or legal/regulatory compliance of the Evergreen Integrated Library System. Without these, the system cannot be deployed or operated safely (e.g., core database transactional integrity, staff authentication, basic catalog search and item checkout).

2. **Should Have (P2 - High Priority)**:
   - Highly important capabilities that provide significant business value, workflow automation, or essential operational reporting. They are not strictly blockers for a minimal prototype, but are required for normal day-to-day production usage across library branches (e.g., automated overnight batch reporting, bulk item transfer interfaces).

3. **Could Have (P3 - Desirable / Medium Priority)**:
   - Useful enhancements, ergonomic interface conveniences, and optional customization features that improve staff efficiency or patron experience if time and development resources permit (e.g., custom color coding on reports, export to obscure file formats).

4. **Won't Have (P4 - Out of Scope / Deferred)**:
   - Features identified during stakeholder discussions that will not be implemented in the current development cycle or that have been deferred to future Evergreen releases.

### Subsystem: Circulation Reporting

#### [REQ-REP-006] Transaction and Circulation Analytics
- **Priority**: `Must Have`
- **Category**: Circulation Reporting
- **Traceability (Source Documents)**: `email_report_features.eml`, `meeting_2009-03-15_minutes.txt`, `survey_results_2009.txt`, `early_draft_srs_galecia.txt`
- **Architectural Rationale**: Detailed transaction analysis is a core management function and is repeatedly identified as a required reporting gap.

**Description & System Behavior:**
The system shall report check-outs, renewals, check-ins, holds placed, holds filled, and related transactions by patron where permitted, branch, library group, system, county, terminal, hour, and other configured dimensions. Check-ins shall distinguish book-drop, terminal, staff, and self-service activity, while check-outs shall distinguish staff, self-service, online, and renewal activity. Check-in date and time reports shall not count renewals as check-ins. Hold reports shall identify the placement method, and results shall support cumulative and individually segmented counts.

### Subsystem: Collection Operations

#### [REQ-COL-005] Uncataloged Material Inventory
- **Priority**: `Should Have`
- **Category**: Collection Operations
- **Traceability (Source Documents)**: `interview_cataloging_2009-05-08.txt`, `early_draft_srs_galecia.txt`
- **Architectural Rationale**: Bulk uncataloged material is a specific operational need that cannot be satisfied by ordinary copy-level cataloging workflows.

**Description & System Behavior:**
The system shall track uncataloged material received in bulk shipments even when individual bibliographic or copy records do not yet exist. Staff shall be able to record quantities, destination branches, shipment or batch identity, distribution status, and subsequent use with minimal per-item processing. Transactions involving this inventory shall be counted in circulation statistics and management reports without being confused with fully cataloged copies. Usage analytics shall support decisions about distribution, cataloging, and continued handling.

#### [REQ-COL-007] Item Transfer Management
- **Priority**: `Must Have`
- **Category**: Collection Operations
- **Traceability (Source Documents)**: `conv_pines018_item_transfer.eml`, `interview_cataloging_2009-05-08.txt`, `early_draft_srs_galecia.txt`
- **Architectural Rationale**: Batch transfer is a repeatedly identified operational capability and must include safeguards because it changes authoritative item locations.

**Description & System Behavior:**
The system shall provide an authorized utility for transferring batches of copies between holding branches. Users shall query and filter candidate copies, save reusable transfer queries, manually select or deselect results, preview proposed changes, update holding-location records, and generate staff pull lists and printable transit labels. Transfers shall preserve original and destination locations, record transfer history, provide alerts for delays or exceptions, and support an auditable undo or compensating action where technically safe. Optional automatic return to the original location shall be supported through configurable time- or event-based rules and a scheduled task.

#### [REQ-COL-008] Missing, Damaged, and In-Transit Reporting
- **Priority**: `Must Have`
- **Category**: Collection Operations
- **Traceability (Source Documents)**: `conv_pines019_020_missing_intransit.eml`, `interview_cataloging_2009-05-08.txt`, `early_draft_srs_galecia.txt`
- **Architectural Rationale**: Missing, damaged, and stalled transit items directly affect inventory control and frontline service.

**Description & System Behavior:**
The system shall provide branch-scoped reports of missing and damaged copies, including condition notes such as water damage or vandalism and configurable date-range filters. It shall also provide a unified report of copies that have remained in transit longer than a configurable threshold, defaulting to seven days. The in-transit report shall combine transit-to and transit-from information, identify relevant branches and due dates, and support sorting by oldest or due date so staff can prioritize follow-up. Reports shall support export and trend analysis where historical events are available.

### Subsystem: Collection Reporting

#### [REQ-COL-001] Material Volume Reporting
- **Priority**: `Must Have`
- **Category**: Collection Reporting
- **Traceability (Source Documents)**: `conv_pines014_material_volume.eml`, `interview_cataloging_2009-05-08.txt`, `early_draft_srs_galecia.txt`
- **Architectural Rationale**: Material volume is a recurring collection-management need and requires both current and historical views.

**Description & System Behavior:**
The system shall report material volume for a selected library, branch, system, or historical point in time. Results shall separately count newly acquired items, circulating items, items currently on the shelf, holds, and the total population, with configurable category breakdowns such as fiction, non-fiction, format, adult fiction, and board books. Users shall be able to filter by date or period, including predefined windows such as 30, 60, and 90 days, and compare historical snapshots. Category definitions shall be configurable without changing the underlying item records.

#### [REQ-COL-002] Shelf Space Utilization Reporting
- **Priority**: `Should Have`
- **Category**: Collection Reporting
- **Traceability (Source Documents)**: `conv_pines016_shelf_space.eml`, `interview_cataloging_2009-05-08.txt`, `early_draft_srs_galecia.txt`
- **Architectural Rationale**: The report supports collection planning, but the estimation method requires validation before it can be treated as a fully reliable operational metric.

**Description & System Behavior:**
The system shall provide shelf-space utilization reports by genre and format, comparing percentage of total circulation, percentage of the collection, and percentage of total shelf space. Reports shall run at system and library or branch scope and provide a side-by-side comparison view for identifying outliers. Users shall be able to apply date ranges where the selected metrics support historical analysis. Because shelf space is not directly recorded, the system shall use a documented estimate based on item dimensions, configured proxies, item count, or another approved method and shall identify the estimation basis in the output.

### Subsystem: Collection Safety

#### [REQ-COL-006] Last Copy Protection Reporting
- **Priority**: `Must Have`
- **Category**: Collection Safety
- **Traceability (Source Documents)**: `conv_pines021_item_purging.eml`, `interview_cataloging_2009-05-08.txt`
- **Architectural Rationale**: Preventing accidental loss of the final consortium copy is a critical collection-integrity safeguard.

**Description & System Behavior:**
The system shall provide a last-copy report identifying titles or bibliographic records for which a selected library, system, or consortium holds the final copy. Users shall be able to choose library-level and system-wide scope and identify the associated copies and holding branches. The report shall be usable before deletion, weeding, transfer, or purging operations. Results shall include sufficient bibliographic and item identifiers to support cataloging integrity and external record maintenance.

#### [REQ-COL-009] Safe Item Weeding and Purging
- **Priority**: `Must Have`
- **Category**: Collection Safety
- **Traceability (Source Documents)**: `conv_pines021_item_purging.eml`, `interview_cataloging_2009-05-08.txt`, `early_draft_srs_galecia.txt`
- **Architectural Rationale**: Purging is inherently destructive and requires strong safeguards, traceability, and last-copy protection.

**Description & System Behavior:**
The system shall identify item and volume records eligible for weeding or purging using combinable criteria such as status, duration in status, circulation inactivity, age, and other activity history. It shall provide read-only candidate reports, last-copy checks, a separate confirmation step, batch status conversion, and an audit trail for every purge or status change. Authorized users may schedule approved purge jobs, but destructive deletion shall not occur without configured safety controls. The system shall support cleanup of orphaned volumes, copies, and records with no attached copies while preserving referential integrity.
